# Gobby Kart

## Language

**Glider**:
The wing a kart opens over a chasm after leaving a glide ramp; flying on it is gliding.
_Avoid_: wing, hang-glider

**Glide ramp**:
The only thing that opens a glider: a blue ramp whose lip drops into a chasm.

**Energy trade**:
The glider's rule that height and speed convert into each other: diving gains speed, climbing spends it.

**Deep water**:
A volume of water karts drive under, switching to the underwater mode; shallow water only changes the road's surface.

**Lap track**:
A track whose main road is a closed loop, raced over several laps.

**Point-to-point track**:
A track whose main road runs once from a start to a separate finish, built around that shape; raced in sections instead of laps.
_Avoid_: one-lap track, sprint

**Section**:
One of the three stretches a point-to-point track is divided into; the HUD shows it as Section 1/3 where a lap track shows LAP 1/3.

**Grip road**:
A stretch of road karts stick to however steeply it banks (up to 80°): in the air over it gravity follows the road's up, and going downhill on it is faster. Not anti-gravity: the road still has a down.
_Avoid_: anti-gravity, wall ride

**Loop**:
A vertical loop spliced into the main road: grip road that climbs over the top and comes back down beside its own entry, shifted sideways by at least the road's width plus 4 m. Karts stick to it at any speed, gravity on it is the road's down, and a hop or a hit on it stays on it.
_Avoid_: loop-the-loop, corkscrew (that's a roll, not a loop)

**Conveyor**:
Dry road that carries karts along or across it, like a current does under deep water.

**Wave**:
A stretch whose road rises and falls in travelling swells, computed from the race tick in the sim and the shader alike.

**Press**:
A mover that slams down onto the road on a cycle and squashes any kart under it.

**Swing**:
A mover that hangs from a pivot and sways across the road; karts ride its deck.

**Cup**:
A set of four tracks raced as one Grand Prix; a track appears in at most one cup.
